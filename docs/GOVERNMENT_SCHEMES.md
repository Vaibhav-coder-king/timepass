# COGNIVA Government Elder-Care Schemes Directory

This document details the 6 official Indian government welfare and health schemes curated in the COGNIVA Government Hub (`/govt-hub`). All scheme data is cached offline for field accessibility by ASHA workers and families.

---

## 1. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (Senior Citizens 70+)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)
- **Coverage**: ₹5,00,000 per year per eligible senior citizen, regardless of family income.
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: **14555**
- **Eligibility**: Any Indian citizen aged 70 years and older.
- **Documents Required**: Aadhaar card with age verification.
- **Official Portal**: https://nha.gov.in

---

## 2. National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly (NPHCE)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- **Benefits**: Dedicated geriatric OPD clinics and 10-bed geriatric wards at all District Hospitals; specialized geriatric beds at Community Health Centres (CHCs).
- **NER Focus**: Strengthened across Assam Medical College (Dibrugarh) and NEIGRIHMS (Shillong).

---

## 3. Tele-MANAS (Tele Mental Health Assistance and Networking Across States)
- **Ministry**: MoHFW / NIMHANS
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: **14416** / **1800-891-4416** (24/7 Free)
- **Benefits**: Immediate tele-counseling and psychiatric support in 20+ regional languages including Assamese, Bengali, and Bodo.

---

## 4. National Helpline for Senior Citizens (Elder Line)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: **14567** (8:00 AM – 8:00 PM Daily)
- **Benefits**: Guidance on elder abuse prevention, legal aid, old-age pension grievances, and geriatric shelter rescue.

---

## 5. Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
- **Benefits**: 100% free distribution of assisted-living devices (walking sticks, hearing aids, wheelchairs, spectacles, artificial dentures) to BPL senior citizens.
- **Agency**: Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India (ALIMCO).

---

## 6. Atal Amrit Abhiyan (Assam)
- **Department**: Health & Family Welfare Department, Government of Assam
- **Benefits**: Cashless treatment up to ₹2,00,000 per individual per year for critical illnesses including neurological conditions, stroke, and cardiovascular ailments.
